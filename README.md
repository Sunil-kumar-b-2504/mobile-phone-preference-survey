# mobile-phone-preference-survey
This project titled “Decoding Mobile Trends: A Data-Driven Analysis of Smartphone Preferences” focuses on studying people’s mobile phone usage habits and buying behavior using data analysis and visualization techniques. A Google Form survey with fifteen questions was designed to collect information about preferred mobile brands, operating systems, daily screen time, price range, key features considered during purchase, and upgrade plans. The survey was shared among friends, classmates, and family members, and more than twenty responses were collected. The responses were downloaded as a CSV file and imported into Python for analysis using Pandas and Matplotlib. Data cleaning, organization, and visualization were carried out to identify trends and preferences clearly through bar charts and pie charts.

The results showed that Samsung and Redmi are the most popular brands among respondents, while Android is the dominant operating system choice. A majority of users spend 4–6 hours daily on their phones, highlighting the strong dependence on mobile devices in daily life. The most common price segment for current phones was found to be between ₹15,000 and ₹30,000, indicating that users prefer mid-range smartphones that deliver performance and value for money. When asked about the main feature influencing their purchase decision, most participants prioritized battery life and camera quality, followed by storage capacity and overall performance.

The analysis also revealed that a significant number of respondents are planning to upgrade their smartphones within the next year, mainly to access better features and technology. Overall, the project successfully demonstrates how survey data can be collected and transformed into meaningful insights using Python. It highlights that today’s smartphone users prefer affordable, feature-rich, and reliable devices that balance cost and performance. Through this project, I gained practical experience in data collection, cleaning, visualization, and interpretation, strengthening my understanding of real-world data analytics and its applications in studying consumer behavior.
